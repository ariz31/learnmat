Teaching sequence
  1. Identify the specimen form and nominal dimensions.
  2. Use the governing geometric relationship.
  3. Orbit the model to confirm the 3D form and surface-area context.
  4. Check the computed volume and area in the HUD.
  5. Do not infer strength, mix design, grade, or certification from appearance.
