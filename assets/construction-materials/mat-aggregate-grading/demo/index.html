Aggregate grading visualization

Compare deliberately illustrative grading distributions using nominal sieve openings and a percent-passing curve. The presets are teaching examples only; no ASTM, AASHTO, DPWH, project, or product specification envelope is encoded.

retainedᵢ = passingᵢ₋₁ − passingᵢ
massᵢ = sample mass × retainedᵢ / 100

Particle circles are decorative symbols, not scaled stones. Percent passing and retained mass are derived from the selected illustrative preset.

Show calculation steps and retained-mass table
  1. Objective: read percent passing and determine how much of the sample is retained between successive sieve openings.
  2. Known data: selected percent-passing preset and sample mass.
  3. Governing relationship: retained fraction equals the drop in percent passing between consecutive sieve openings; the pan receives the fraction passing the smallest modeled sieve.
  4. Check: retained fractions including pan must total 100%, and retained masses must recover the original sample mass.
  5. Conclusion: the curve describes this illustrative distribution only; compare shape and retained fractions, not specification compliance.
