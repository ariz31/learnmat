Masonry unit geometry and bond

Explore a generic hollow masonry unit and a schematic running-bond arrangement. Dimensions and voids are illustrative geometry only; they do not identify a standard unit, structural capacity, fire rating, mortar specification, or code-compliant wall detail.

The model treats voids as rectangular prisms extending through the full unit height. Face shells, webs, tapers, ribs, reinforcement, mortar behavior, and code checks are outside scope.
