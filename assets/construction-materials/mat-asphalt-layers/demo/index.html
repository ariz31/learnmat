Show calculation steps and layer volumes
  1. Objective: relate individual lift thicknesses to total specimen thickness and layer volume.
  2. Known data: three layer thicknesses plus slab plan dimensions or core diameter.
  3. Governing relationships: total thickness is the sum of the three layers; layer volume equals cross-sectional/plan area times layer thickness.
  4. Intermediate work: inspect each layer thickness fraction and geometric volume in the table.
  5. Check: layer thicknesses must sum exactly to total thickness, and layer volumes must sum to the displayed sample volume.
  6. Conclusion: changing geometry changes proportions and volume only; it does not establish a valid pavement section or material performance.
