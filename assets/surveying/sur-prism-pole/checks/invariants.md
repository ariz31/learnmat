# Invariants

- Pole base remains fixed at local (0,0,0).
- Prism center is `(H sin θ, H cos θ, 0)`.
- H=2.0 m and θ=0 gives prism center (0,2.0,0).
- H=2.0 m and θ=5° gives horizontal offset about 0.17431 m and vertical projection about 1.99239 m.
- The magnitude from base to prism center remains exactly H for any permitted tilt.
- Reduced-motion preference affects only illustrative person breathing.
- Parameter validation occurs before state replacement.
