# Invariants

- Staff base remains at local (0, 0, 0).
- Top point for staff height H and tilt θ is (H sin θ, H cos θ, 0).
- For H=3 m and θ=0, top point is (0, 3, 0).
- For H=3 m and θ=5°, horizontal top offset is approximately 0.2615 m and vertical projection approximately 2.9886 m.
- A requested reading height greater than staff height is rejected before state mutation.
- Reduced-motion preference affects only illustrative person breathing, not staff geometry.
